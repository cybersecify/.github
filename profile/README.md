## What We Do

Two service lines. That is the whole business.

**Penetration Testing.** Manual exploitation by a named tester, not scanner output. Surfaces we test: web applications, APIs (REST, GraphQL, webhooks), AI and LLM applications (agents, tool calling, RAG, MCP servers), Android, iOS, cloud (AWS, Azure, GCP), IoT, internal and external networks, desktop and Electron. Every engagement is run by the two founders personally.

**Audit and Compliance Readiness.** SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness, per finding control mapping, and the evidence an auditor will accept. We prepare and evidence; the certifying audit itself is issued by a separate accredited auditor.

Common reasons buyers come to us: a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit, an enterprise customer security questionnaire, investor diligence, or a platform programme requirement.

**Platform programme pentests.** We deliver the penetration testing leg of the Amazon SP-API Data Protection Policy (Section 2.7.2), Microsoft 365 Certification, and the Shopify Partner protected customer data requirements, indexed for Shopify against the Level 1 and Level 2 control lists. These are ungated requirements: no empanelment and no vendor certification is needed, only the client's written authorisation.

## Engagement Model

- **Startup plan, INR 74,999.** 1 scope, 5 business days, 6 hours of founder led consulting, 1 free retest. A second scope is INR 44,999, and this plan caps at 2 scopes.
- **Growth plan, INR 1,79,999.** 2 scopes, 10 business days, 12 hours of founder led consulting, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 control mapping, a signed Letter of Attestation, 1 free retest. Additional scopes are INR 74,999 each, with no scope limit.
- Scopes run sequentially by default, so each scope adds 5 business days. Estates of 5 or more scopes get a custom scoping proposal.
- Retest is included in both plans. It runs within one month of the v1.0 report, and sooner once the client's fixes are in. The retest deliverable is a v2.0 report.

## Open Source: OpenEASD

[![CI](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD/actions/workflows/ci.yml) [![CodeQL](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD/actions/workflows/codeql.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD/actions/workflows/codeql.yml) [![Docker Image](https://img.shields.io/badge/ghcr.io-latest-2496ed?logo=docker&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD/pkgs/container/openeasd) [![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD/blob/main/LICENSE) [![GitHub Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cybersecify/OpenEASD?style=social)](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD/stargazers)

[**OpenEASD**](https://github.com/cybersecify/OpenEASD) is our free, MIT licensed external attack surface discovery tool. It finds internet facing assets, scores what is wrong with them, and on a rescan tells you what is new and what is gone since the last run.

It wraps the standard OSS recon tools (`subfinder`, `amass`, `alterx`, `dnsx`, `subzy`, `naabu`, `httpx`, `gau`, `waybackurls`, `katana`, `nuclei`, `nmap` and `cloud_enum`) behind a single web UI with scheduling, alerts and findings tracking. No telemetry. Results stay on your machine.

Self host the Docker image and run unlimited scans on your own infrastructure, or use the free hosted snapshot at [cybersecify.com/openeasd](https://cybersecify.com/openeasd/). There is no paid tier and no upsell on the tool itself. Sample output: [cybersecify.com/sample-openeasd-report](https://cybersecify.com/sample-openeasd-report/).
